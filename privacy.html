Scripture Streak

Privacy Policy

Last updated 28 October 2026

Scripture Streak is made by QuickQuote Group PTY LTD, an Australian company ("we", "us"). In short: everything you enter stays on your phone, we never receive it, and there are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking.

What stays on your phone

Your reading plan and progress, the days you read, your highlights and notes, the Bible you read and your reminder time are stored only on your device (and in your own iPhone backups). We never receive any of it.

Downloading a Bible

Ten Bibles come with the app. When you download another one, the app fetches it from eBible.org, which publishes free public domain and openly licensed Bibles. Like any website, eBible.org sees the request, including your internet address; nothing else about you is sent. The More Apps screen asks Apple's App Store which of our apps are available, in the same way.

Purchases

Scripture Streak Pro is bought through Apple. Apple handles the payment and tells the app whether Pro is active. We don't receive your name, email or payment details.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share your information, show ads, use analytics, or track you across other apps and websites.

Children

The app collects no information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no information about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. Deleting the app removes everything it stored. If you have a question or complaint, email us. If you're in Australia you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner; if you're in the EU or UK, your local data protection authority.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above.
