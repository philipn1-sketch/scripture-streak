Scripture Streak

Help and support

Questions, problems or a verse that looks wrong? Email admin@quick-quote.com.au and we'll get back to you.

Reading each day

The home screen shows today's reading from your plan. Tap it to read, then tap Mark day read when you finish. The Gospels plan is free; choose another plan in Reading plans.

Your streak

Your streak counts the days in a row you finished a reading. Today doesn't break it until the day is over. With Pro, up to 2 missed days a month are bridged by a streak freeze.

Bibles and languages

Ten Bibles come with the app, including English, Spanish, French, German and Latin. In Bible version you can download more than seventy free Bibles in over thirty languages from eBible.org. If your phone's language has a Bible, the app offers it.

Reminders

Turn on the daily reminder in Settings and choose the time. If reminders don't arrive, check that notifications for Scripture Streak are allowed in your iPhone's Settings.

Scripture Streak Pro

Pro adds Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets, every reading plan, streak freezes, highlights and notes on any verse, and your reading history. Restore a purchase or manage your subscription in Settings.
